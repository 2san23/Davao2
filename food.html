Food and Delicacies

Flavors of Davao del Sur

Durian

The famous creamy fruit loved for its rich flavor and strong aroma.

Try it at: Bankerohan Market and local fruit stands

Tablea and Cacao

Davao cacao is used for hot chocolate, desserts, and award-winning local chocolate.

Try it at: Malagos and pasalubong shops

Grilled Tuna

Fresh seafood grilled simply and served with local sauces and rice.

Try it at: Seaside restaurants and night markets

Kinilaw

A refreshing Filipino ceviche made with fresh fish, vinegar, ginger, onion, and chili.

Try it at: Local eateries and seafood restaurants
