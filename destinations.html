Mount Apo

Santa Cruz / Digos City

Mountain

The country's highest peak, popular for trekking, camping, and sunrise views.

  • Entrance: Guide and permit fees vary by trail
  • Hours: By permit and scheduled climb

Mount Apo is the highest mountain in the Philippines and one of Mindanao's most iconic adventure destinations. Climbers visit for mossy forests, boulder trails, volcanic landscapes, and sweeping views from the summit.

Trekking, camping, birdwatching, landscape photography

Dry season, especially February to May

Book an accredited guide, prepare physically, pack warm layers, and follow leave-no-trace rules.

Philippine Eagle Center

Davao City

Adventure

A conservation center for the Philippine eagle and other native wildlife.

  • Entrance: Entrance fee applies
  • Hours: Usually daytime hours

The Philippine Eagle Center is a learning and conservation destination where visitors can see the critically endangered Philippine eagle and understand the importance of forest protection.

Wildlife viewing, education tours, photography

Morning visits for cooler weather

Keep noise low, avoid flash photography near animals, and support conservation programs.

Malagos Garden Resort

Davao City

Food Destination

A garden resort known for cacao, chocolate, nature, and family activities.

  • Entrance: Entrance and activity fees vary
  • Hours: Usually daytime resort hours

Malagos Garden Resort combines gardens, bird encounters, cacao experiences, and local chocolate products, making it a favorite family and food destination.

Chocolate tasting, garden walks, bird encounters, dining

Weekdays or early weekends

Try local chocolate products and check package inclusions before visiting.

Davao Crocodile Park

Davao City

Adventure

A wildlife park featuring crocodiles, animal exhibits, and educational shows.

  • Entrance: Entrance fee applies
  • Hours: Usually daytime to early evening

Davao Crocodile Park is a wildlife attraction where families can learn about crocodiles, reptiles, birds, and other animals through exhibits and scheduled activities.

Animal viewing, educational shows, family tours

Late afternoon for cooler weather

Check show schedules and supervise children near viewing areas.

Dawis Beach

Digos City

Beach

A local coastal spot for simple sea views, walks, and casual relaxation.

  • Entrance: May vary by area or cottage use
  • Hours: Daytime recommended

Dawis Beach offers a relaxed coastal atmosphere with open views and local seaside charm. It is a good stop for visitors looking for a quiet beachside break.

Beach walks, picnics, sunset viewing

Morning or late afternoon

Beach

An accessible coastal area with local seafood and laid-back sea views.

  • Entrance: Depends on resort or access point
  • Hours: Varies by establishment

Talomo Beach is known as an easy coastal escape near the city, suitable for simple seaside meals, quick visits, and relaxed gatherings.

Samal Island

Island Garden City of Samal

Island

An island escape near Davao with beaches, resorts, caves, and clear waters.

  • Entrance: Ferry and resort fees vary
  • Hours: Ferry schedules and resort hours vary

Samal Island is a favorite side trip from Davao for beach resorts, snorkeling, island-hopping, cave visits, and relaxing seaside stays.

Swimming, snorkeling, island hopping, resort stays

Dry months and weekdays

Historical Site

A wartime tunnel attraction connected to local World War II history.

  • Entrance: Entrance fee may apply
  • Hours: Usually daytime visitor hours

The Japanese Tunnel offers a short historical stop where visitors can learn about wartime stories and see displays connected to Davao's past.

History tour, photo stops, heritage learning

Any clear day

Roxas Avenue Night Market

Davao City

Food Destination

A lively evening destination for street food, snacks, and affordable finds.

  • Entrance: Free entry; pay for food and items
  • Hours: Evening hours

Roxas Avenue Night Market is popular for grilled food, local snacks, budget shopping, and an energetic evening atmosphere.

Tudaya Falls

Santa Cruz

Waterfall

A scenic waterfall destination for nature trips and outdoor exploration.

  • Entrance: Fees may vary depending on guide or access point
  • Hours: Daytime visits recommended

Tudaya Falls is a natural attraction associated with the Mount Apo area, visited by travelers looking for cool mountain scenery, forest surroundings, and a quieter outdoor stop.

Nature viewing, photography, guided outdoor trips
