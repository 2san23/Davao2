Festivals and Culture

Celebrations Worth Planning Around

Kadayawan Festival

Davao City | August

A thanksgiving festival celebrating harvest, indigenous culture, floral abundance, music, dances, and the unity of Davao communities.

Araw ng Dabaw

Davao City | March

A city anniversary celebration with parades, cultural showcases, sports events, pageantry, trade fairs, and community performances.
